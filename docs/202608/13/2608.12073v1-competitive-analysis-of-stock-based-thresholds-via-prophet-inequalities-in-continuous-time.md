## 摘要
我们研究了一个连续时间的 $K$ 个单位在线资源分配问题，该问题具有非齐次泊松到达和时变估值分布。尽管最优动态策略通常同时依赖于剩余库存和剩余计划时间，我们聚焦于一类更简单且在实际中更具吸引力的基于库存的阈值策略，其有限数量的阈值仅取决于剩余单位数量。我们以多单位先知基准来评估这些策略，该基准在事后选择 $K$ 个已实现价值中的最佳值。我们的主要贡献是建立了一个针对连续时间基于库存阈值策略的新竞争分析框架。我们首先通过类型覆盖对偶问题重新表述该问题。分析该对偶问题的核心挑战在于，该对偶问题既无限维又非凸：对手可以选择时变的到达过程和估值过程，而策略性能非线性地依赖于随机库存轨迹。我们通过将连续时间对抗问题归约为泊松优化问题 $PoisOPTRe_K$，并证明其最坏情况解的精确结构性质，克服了这些挑战。特别地，对抗性到达具有截断与延迟填充结构，这使得对两个阈值情形可得到精确的四参数表示，对一般阈值情形可得到有限的嵌套区间表示。这些归约使得所得到的保证可直接计算。例如，对于两个阈值，当 $K=2$ 时我们得到竞争比 $0.6269$；对于三个阈值，当 $K=3$ 时我们得到竞争比 $0.6816$。通过这种方式，我们表明简单的基于库存阈值策略尽管忽略日历时间，仍能实现强先知不等式保证。

## Abstract
We study a continuous-time $K$-unit online resource allocation problem with nonhomogeneous Poisson arrivals and time-varying valuation distributions. While the optimal dynamic policy generally depends on both the remaining inventory and the time left in the horizon, we focus on a simpler and practically appealing class of stock-based threshold policies, whose limited number of thresholds depend only on the number of units remaining. We evaluate these policies against the multi-unit prophet benchmark, which selects the best $K$ realized values in hindsight. Our main contribution is a new competitive-analysis framework for stock-based thresholds in continuous time. We first reformulate the problem through a type-covering dual. The central challenge for analyzing the dual is that the dual is both infinite-dimensional and non-convex: the adversary can choose time-varying arrival and valuation processes, while the policy performance depends nonlinearly on the stochastic inventory trajectory.   We overcome these challenges by reducing the continuous-time adversarial problem to a Poisson optimization $PoisOPTRe_K$, and then proving sharp structural properties of its worst-case solutions. In particular, adversarial arrivals admit cutoff and late-filling structures, which yield an exact four-parameter formulation for two thresholds and a finite nested-interval representation for general thresholds. These reductions make the guarantees directly computable. For example, for two thresholds, we obtain a competitive ratio $0.6269$ for $K=2$; with three thresholds, we obtain the ratio $0.6816$ for $K=3$. In this way, we show that simple stock-based thresholds achieve strong prophet-inequality guarantees despite ignoring calendar time.
